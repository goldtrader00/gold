Small print. "Contracts open" (open interest) = option contracts that exist and have not been closed; it is a map of where traders have positioned, not a buy/sell signal. Each zone is one GLD strike wide (about $11 of gold) and the conversion to gold prices can be off by roughly $5–15, so treat edges as approximate.
