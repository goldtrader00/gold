Small print. "Contracts open" (open interest) = option contracts that exist and have not been closed; it is a map of where traders have positioned, not a buy/sell signal. Each zone is one GLD strike wide (about $11 of gold) and the conversion to gold prices can be off by roughly $5–15, so treat edges as approximate. The Smart Money score is an approximate reconstruction (0.7 × swap-dealer index + 0.5 × options premium skew), unvalidated; the options-flow card can be skewed by in-the-money puts after a drop, so I would ignore both.
